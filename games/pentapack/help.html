Rules and controls

Fill every playable cell of the board with the provided pentominoes. Each piece has five connected cells. Pieces may rotate and reflect, but cannot overlap or cover holes in the board. Drag a piece from the piece tray into the board; drag it back to remove it. Double-click or double-tap a piece, including one in the tray, to rotate 90 degrees. Right-click or hold for about half a second to mirror it. An invalid placement snaps back without costing a move. U: undo; H: solution-guided hint; R: restart; V: mirror the last active piece; Escape: dismiss the result card. 匣阵 = campaign; 每日 = daily; 随机 = seeded random challenge. 撤销 = undo; 提示 = hint; 倒回 = restart. The built-in ? button opens the original tutorial. Completed records and campaign unlocks are saved, but unfinished placements restart on reload.

Interface and saves

The original interface is Simplified Chinese. This guide is not a full translation. Saves are local to this browser when storage is available. Each game has its own namespaced keys; storage failures should not prevent play. Fullscreen may be unavailable in an iframe and is not required.
