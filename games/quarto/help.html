Rules

Choose a piece for your opponent, then place the piece they give you on an empty square. Four pieces in a row, column or long diagonal win if they share at least one attribute: tall/short, light/dark, round/square, solid/hollow. The optional 2×2 variant also counts square blocks. A full board with no winning line is a draw.

Controls and progress

Select Computer or Two players in New game. In computer mode choose a strength and who starts. The highlighted hand shows the piece you must place. Undo, restart, rules and theme controls are available. Piece attributes have text labels as well as shapes. Only settings persist; reloading starts a new game.

Browser storage is optional; records cannot persist if it is blocked or unavailable. All runtime code and assets are local. No account or network service is needed.
