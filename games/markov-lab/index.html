A word-level Markov chain trained on a small built-in corpus. The next word depends only on the last few words — enough to fake the rhythm of English, not enough to understand it.

Order 1 remembers one word, so it rambles. Order 2 and 3 remember phrases — the text gets more coherent but starts copying the corpus verbatim.

Keys 1–3 set the order, G generates, R retrains on a fresh seed. Temperature above 1 flattens the distribution (wilder text); below 1 sharpens it (safer text).
