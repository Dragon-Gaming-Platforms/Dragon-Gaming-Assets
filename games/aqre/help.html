Aqre

← Play Aqre

Exact rules

  1. Thick borders divide the grid into rooms. A small number tells how many cells in that room must be shaded.
  2. The numbered cell is not a fixed white cell: it may be shaded too.
  3. All shaded cells must form one nonempty group through shared edges. Diagonal contact alone does not connect cells.
  4. No four consecutive cells in a horizontal or vertical line may be all shaded or all clear. This rule crosses room borders.

Controls and progress

Tap a cell to cycle undecided → shaded → clear → undecided. Use the Brush menu to paint only one state, or choose Undecided to erase. Right-click marks a cell clear. Focus a cell and use 1 for shaded, 0 for clear, Backspace/Delete to erase, and arrow keys to move. Space activates the focused cell.

Undo restores the last edit, including a hint edit, but never refunds moves or hint usage. Undo history lasts for this visit only. Restart starts a fresh attempt at the same puzzle; the level selector and Next puzzle move between the 18 original puzzles. All puzzles are selectable from the start.

Check evaluates the rules without looking up the answer. On a partial board, “no contradiction” does not mean every choice is correct. A Hint explicitly reveals one answer cell, correcting a marked mistake first; it is not a human-deduction explanation. Hinted completions are labeled assisted. Records rank fewer hints, then fewer moves, then time.

The clock pauses while the page is hidden, when paused, and after a win. Optional local storage remembers the current board, time, moves, hints and per-puzzle records. Invalid or unavailable storage falls back safely. Clear local progress resets only this game’s key. No accounts, server APIs, CDNs or service workers.
