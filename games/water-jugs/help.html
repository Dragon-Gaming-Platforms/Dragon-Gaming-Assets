Rules and controls

Measure exactly the target amount in the specified jug. Drag a jug to the faucet to fill it, to the drain to empty it, or onto another jug to transfer water until the source empties or the destination fills. Dragging from the faucet onto a jug also fills it. Partial transfers are not allowed. Actions that change no water do not count.

The panel shows capacities, target, moves and a proven minimum. H / 提示 shows the next move only while you remain on the supplied certified route; it does not solve arbitrary off-route positions. U / 撤销 undoes; R / 重来 restarts. Exact par with no hints earns the best rating. Campaign records, not unfinished water levels, are saved.

Interface and saves

The original interface is Simplified Chinese; this guide is not a full translation. Campaign / 战役 selects regular puzzles; 每日 selects a date-based puzzle; 随机 selects a seeded challenge. Browser storage is game-scoped and optional. Storage denial or quota failures must not prevent play; progress cannot persist when storage is unavailable. Fullscreen may be unavailable in an iframe and is not required.
