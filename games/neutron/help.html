Exact rules

  1. Blue has five pawns on the TOP row, Amber five on the BOTTOM row, and the neutral N starts in the center. Blue starts. The opening exception: Blue slides only an own pawn, not the neutron.
  2. After the opening, slide the neutral N first, then slide one of your own pawns. A slide travels horizontally, vertically or diagonally AS FAR AS POSSIBLE until the next step would hit a piece or board edge. You may not stop early.
  3. If the neutron reaches the top row, Blue wins; if it reaches the bottom row, Amber wins. The owner of that home row wins even when the opponent moved it there. No pawn slide follows a winning neutron slide.
  4. If a player cannot slide the neutron at the start of their turn, that player loses. In this edition, having no own pawn slide after moving the neutron also loses. There are no captures or passes.
  5. Anti-loop house rules: three occurrences of the same completed-turn board and player to move, or 200 completed turns, produce a draw.

Controls and computer play

Select the neutral N or an allowed own pawn, then tap a marked slide endpoint; intermediate squares are not legal stopping points. Escape or Cancel selection clears the selection. Cancel turn during the pawn stage restores the whole turn to its start.

Native cell buttons support Tab, Enter and Space; arrow keys navigate without wrapping rows. Cell labels announce coordinates, contents and legal destinations. Blue stones are round/B-marked, Amber stones rounded squares/A-marked, and the neutral N is gold and unowned.

Choose local two-player or computer play, your color and a computer level, then press Start new match. Menus alone do not change the active match. Blue always starts. Casual chooses a random legal complete turn. Tactical scores at most 512 evenly sampled complete turns using a one-ply game-specific space/center heuristic (plus immediate-threat guards in Squava/Neutron). It is not deep, optimal or expert play. Suggest turn shows that same policy without making the move.

Undo, pause and saving

Undo restores the current unfinished turn, or one full turn in local play. Against the computer it restores your previous decision through the computer’s reply. Undo works after results and reload. Before your first decision, the computer’s opening cannot be undone; use Start new match. Pause covers the board and cancels scheduled computer play; hidden pages defer computer moves.

Only this game’s key stores settings plus a legal action replay, including an unfinished neutron/pawn turn. Every saved action is revalidated from the original setup on reload, rebuilding undo history. Invalid/corrupt saves start fresh; denied storage remains playable. Clear saved match affects only this game and starts fresh with selected settings. No accounts, online opponents, external runtime services, timers or rankings.
