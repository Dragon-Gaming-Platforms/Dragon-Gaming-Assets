Exact edition rules

  1. This edition plays on a 7×7 grid of points, displayed as clickable cells. Blue starts; players alternate placing one own-color stone on an empty point.
  2. A group is a chain of same-color stones connected horizontally or vertically. A liberty is an empty point sharing an edge with any stone of that group. Diagonal contact does not connect groups.
  3. Both capture and suicide are forbidden: after a placement, EVERY group of either color must have at least one liberty. No stones are removed.
  4. No passing. If the next player has no legal placement, that player loses. There is no territory scoring or ko rule; the board only fills.

Controls

Tap a marked empty point to place your stone. Native buttons support Tab, Enter and Space; arrows navigate the board without horizontal row wrapping. Escape or Cancel selection clears a selection or suggestion. Cell labels announce coordinates, contents and legal destinations. A liberty is an orthogonally adjacent empty point. Diagonals do not connect groups.

Local and computer play

Select local two-player or computer play, your role and a level, then press Start new match. Menu changes alone do not alter the active match. The first listed role always starts. Casual picks a random legal action. Tactical: One-ply mobility heuristic; not expert or optimal play. NoGo scores own minus opponent legal mobility after every candidate. Other games inspect every opponent reply for terminal results and sample at most 1,600 nonterminal reply heuristics in total, using forward progress/material in Knightthrough and unmixed five-cell windows in Order and Chaos. Traffic Lights and Notakto use terminal avoidance only, not deep solvers. Suggest turn displays that same policy without playing it.

Undo, pause and local saving

Undo restores one move in local play, or your last decision through the computer reply in computer play. Undo works after results and reload. A computer opening before your first decision cannot be undone; start a new match instead. Pause covers the board and stops scheduled computer play. Hidden pages defer computer turns.

Only this game's key stores settings plus a legal action replay. Every action, including the selected X/O mark, is revalidated from the initial board on reload, rebuilding undo history. Corrupt or illegal saves start fresh. Denied storage remains playable for this visit. Clear saved match affects only this game and starts fresh with selected settings. No accounts, online opponents, external services or timers.
