Rules and controls

Draw a single closed orthogonal loop through cell centers. It cannot branch, cross itself or use a cell twice. The loop must pass through every black dot; each dot is exactly halfway along the maximal straight segment through it, measured between turns. A dot on an edge requires that edge to be used. Drag from one cell center to its neighbor to draw. Right-click an edge to mark it excluded. Mark unused edges around dots and beyond the ends of their straight segments: the checker needs these exclusions to confirm the midpoint. On touchscreens choose the exclusion pen (排除叉) and tap the midpoint of the edge you want to exclude. Buttons: 画环 = draw loop; 排除叉 = exclude edge; 擦掉 = erase; 提示 = hint; 检查 = check; 撤销 = undo; 全清 = clear board; 换一局 = new puzzle. E cycles the pen; arrow keys and Enter can draw from the cursor. H: hint; C: check; Z: undo; N: new. Choose a size at the top. Larger puzzles can take a while to generate.

Interface and saves

The original interface is Simplified Chinese. This English guide is an adaptation, not a full translation. Progress is saved locally in this browser when storage is available. Clearing this game's save does not clear other games. Fullscreen may be unavailable in an embedded player; the game does not require it.
