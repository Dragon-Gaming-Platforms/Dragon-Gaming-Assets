Rules and controls

The numbers on the piles are their card counts. Deal removes one card from every nonempty pile and combines those removed cards into a new pile; empty piles disappear. You may also merge two piles, or split a pile into two positive piles. These extra operations are part of this edition, so its shortest-route target is not the same as the number of deals alone.

Reach the target multiset shown on the page; pile order does not matter. With a triangular total, the target is a staircase of sizes k, k−1, …, 1. Nontriangular boards use a designated member of their repeating deal cycle. Par is the shortest number of actions using all three operations; the separate deals-only count is not par.

Use Deal for one deal. Tap one pile then another to merge them. Drag downward from an internal card in a pile to split it at that card. D/Enter/Space deals; number keys 1–9 select piles. Undo reverses the last action; Hint points out a shortest-route action. Campaign, daily and seeded random modes are available. Records persist when storage is available; a reload restarts the board rather than restoring its exact intermediate piles.

Interface and storage

This is not a full translation: menus, diagnostics and hint prose remain partly Simplified Chinese. Browser storage is optional and scoped to this game. Records cannot persist if storage is unavailable. Fullscreen is optional and may be unavailable in an iframe.
