Rules and controls

A numbered arrow counts all black cells along its ray to the board edge. Clue cells cannot be shaded or crossed by the loop. Black cells cannot share an edge. Connect cell centers to make exactly one nonbranching, noncrossing loop through every non-black, non-clue cell. There are no unused gray cells in this version. The loop does not have to pass through every row or column.

Use the on-screen Line / Black / Exclude tools. Drag between neighboring cell centers to draw loop edges; tap a cell in Black mode to shade it. Clues are fixed. Crosses are optional notes, not loop edges. Hint places the next forced mark when it is consistent with your board; a conflicting mark must be undone first. Undo reverts a marking action but still counts as an action. New creates a new puzzle; an unfinished board is restored automatically on reload.

Interface and storage

This is not a full translation: menus, diagnostics and hint prose remain partly Simplified Chinese. Browser storage is optional and scoped to this game. Records cannot persist if storage is unavailable. Fullscreen is optional and may be unavailable in an iframe.
