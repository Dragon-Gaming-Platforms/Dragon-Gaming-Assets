English draughts. You are red and move first.

Rules

English draughts

  1. Red moves first. Men move one square diagonally forward; kings move one square in any diagonal direction.
  2. Capturing is mandatory. Jump over an adjacent opponent piece onto an empty square; men may also jump backward.
  3. A jump can continue in the same turn to capture more pieces. There is no maximum-capture rule.
  4. A man that ends its move on the far row becomes a king. Win by capturing every opposing piece or leaving the opponent no legal move.

Click a red piece, then a highlighted square. The computer answers after each move. Undo takes back your move and the computer’s reply. Medium and Hard search several moves ahead, so the computer may take a moment.

Checkers rules

  1. Red moves first; men move forward diagonally, kings move in any diagonal direction.
  2. Capturing is mandatory; men may capture backward as well.
  3. Jumps may continue in the same turn.
  4. Reaching the far row makes a king. Leave the opponent with no legal move to win.
