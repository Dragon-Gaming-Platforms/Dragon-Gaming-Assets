Rules of ZenLITS (ＬＩＴＳ)

In Japanese timber joinery (Kigumi 木組み), craft harmonious balance by partitioning every chamber of the temple:

1. One Tetromino Per Chamber

Every outlined room must contain exactly 4 shaded timber blocks forming a valid tetromino.

2. Four Sacred Shapes (L, I, T, S)

The 4 shaded blocks must form an L, I, T, or S (rotations and reflections allowed). Solid 2×2 "O" blocks are strictly forbidden!

3. No Touching Identical Shapes

If two tetrominoes in adjacent rooms touch orthogonally (sharing an edge), they cannot be of the same type (e.g. an L cannot touch another L). Diagonal contact is allowed.

4. No 2×2 Solid Squares

Shaded blocks cannot form a 2×2 solid square anywhere across the temple floor.

5. Single Connected Network

All shaded blocks across the entire temple must connect orthogonally into a single unbroken structure.

Controls & Tips

  • Tap or Drag across cells to paint timber blocks or mark empty floor dots.
  • Use 💡 Hint for deductive guidance whenever you need insight into forced placements or 2×2 avoidance.
  • Chamber glyphs automatically glow serene green when their tetromino is valid and conflict-free!
