Theory—

With private values drawn uniformly and symmetric risk-neutral bidders, first-price, second-price and English auctions all raise the same expected revenue — the revenue equivalence theorem. The bars show one auction; the chart shows the average of thousands.

Space redraws values, R reruns the sample, 1–3 switch rules. Push shading below equilibrium and watch first-price revenue fall while second price ignores it entirely.
