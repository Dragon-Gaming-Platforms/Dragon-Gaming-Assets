A Bézier curve always stays inside the convex hull of its control points, its endpoints are the first and last control points, and its degree is one less than the number of points. Drag a point to see all three hold.

Click anywhere on the canvas to move the parameter there. Keys 1–3 load the presets, H toggles the hull, D toggles de Casteljau.
