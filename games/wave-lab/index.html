Pluck at the centre and the even harmonics vanish — their nodes sit exactly where your finger is. Move the pluck and a different set of harmonics sounds.

Drag on the string to set the pluck point. Keys 1–4 load presets, Space plucks. Try plucking at 0.33 L: every third harmonic disappears.
