Rules and controls

Houses are ordered left to right, numbered 1 through N. Each house has one item from every category, and every item is used exactly once. Apply the English clues to deduce all assignments. “Directly left/right” means immediately next door; “somewhere left/right” need not be adjacent. “Not next door” also permits two items in the same house. A clue with n houses between means a distance of n+1.

The canvas compares a pair of categories. Tap to cycle blank → cross → check. Cross means excluded; check means a match. Arrows navigate, X excludes, O checks, Space cycles, Delete clears; [ and ] change category pairs. Row/column labels abbreviate names to three letters; full names appear in clues and answer menus. Category navigation: 人 = Person, 颜色 = Color, 宠物 = Pet, 饮料 = Drink, 职业 = Job, 爱好 = Hobby.

Notes are for deduction, not the submitted answer. In the lower answer panel choose every house’s item in each category, then use Check answer. A complete valid assignment wins even if some notes remain blank. 提示 gives a logical deduction; 清空注记 clears this puzzle’s notes. Tier and seed controls generate reproducible puzzles; a changed seed must be loaded with the adjacent button. The interface and hint prose remain partly Chinese. Player notes/seed are saved, but answer dropdowns must be completed again after reload.

Interface and saves

The original interface is Simplified Chinese; this guide is not a full translation. Campaign / 战役 selects regular puzzles; 每日 selects a date-based puzzle; 随机 selects a seeded challenge. Browser storage is game-scoped and optional. Storage denial or quota failures must not prevent play; progress cannot persist when storage is unavailable. Fullscreen may be unavailable in an iframe and is not required.
