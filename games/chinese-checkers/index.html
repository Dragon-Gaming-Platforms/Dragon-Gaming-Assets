Chinese Checkers

Race ten marbles across the star. First to fill the far point wins.

Rules

Reach the far point

  1. Red moves first, then Blue. Each side starts with ten marbles filling one point of the star.
  2. On your turn, move one marble to an adjacent empty hole, or hop over an adjacent marble into the empty hole beyond it. Hops can be chained within one turn, and you may stop after any hop.
  3. The first side to move all ten marbles into the opposite point wins.
  4. 300 moves without a winner is a draw.

Click a marble to select it. Highlighted holes are reachable. Keyboard: Tab to a hole, then Enter.
