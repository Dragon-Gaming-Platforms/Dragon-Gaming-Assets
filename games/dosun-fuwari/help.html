Rules and controls

Fixed black cells are obstacles, not regions. In each outlined region, place exactly one balloon (light circle) and one iron ball (dark circle). Regions do not block vertical support. A balloon must have a balloon or fixed black cell directly above it, or touch the top edge. An iron ball must have an iron ball or fixed black cell directly below it, or touch the bottom edge. An opposite-type ball cannot provide support.

Tap a usable cell to cycle its mark, or select it and choose Balloon / Ball / Empty / Unknown. Empty means a deliberately unused cell, not unknown. All usable cells must be decided to complete a board. Hint describes a deduction without placing a ball. Apply its advice with the marking tools; Undo reverts a marking action. Records are time-only and do not penalize hints. Pause freezes the timer and locks the board. Choose a tier on the menu, and Resume an unfinished board from there.

Interface and storage

This is not a full translation: menus, diagnostics and hint prose remain partly Simplified Chinese. Browser storage is optional and scoped to this game. Records cannot persist if storage is unavailable. Fullscreen is optional and may be unavailable in an iframe.
