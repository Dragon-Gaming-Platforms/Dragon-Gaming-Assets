Rules and controls

On your turn, take k squares of side b from the long edge of an a × b rectangle, where 1 ≤ k ≤ floor(a/b). The remainder is turned so its long side is a again. Whoever takes the last square wins. The computer replies automatically with perfect play.

Tap a block boundary, drag to preview a cut then release, or use a k= button. A larger k cuts more squares in this one turn; the full-width button is not necessarily the winning choice. The live English panel reports dimensions, legal k values, whose turn it is and the result. Arrows adjust the preview; Enter commits. U undoes one player/computer round; H reveals advice and counts as help; R restarts. Campaign, daily and seeded random modes are available. Demonstrate plays out the table strategy and counts as help. Completed records persist, not unfinished cuts.

Interface and saves

This guide is not a full translation. Some menus, diagnostic panels and hint prose remain Simplified Chinese. Browser storage is optional and game-scoped; progress cannot persist if storage is blocked or full. Fullscreen is optional and may be unavailable in an iframe.
