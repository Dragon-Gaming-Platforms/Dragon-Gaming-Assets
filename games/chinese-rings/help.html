Rules and controls

Remove every ring from the sword. Ring 1 (the lowest-numbered ring) can always toggle. Ring k, for k ≥ 2, can toggle only when ring k−1 is on the sword and all lower-numbered rings before k−1 are off. Grey rings are currently immovable; invalid taps cost no moves.

Each legal toggle counts as one move. The displayed minimum comes from exhaustive search over all configurations, not a guessed solution. H / 提示 highlights the optimal next ring; U / 撤销 undoes; R / 重来 restarts. D / 演示 starts a solver demonstration. Stars and board badges measure reaching par; the lifetime clean tally excludes hints and demonstrations. Completed records are saved, not unfinished ring positions.

Interface and saves

The original interface is Simplified Chinese; this guide is not a full translation. Campaign / 战役 selects regular puzzles; 每日 selects a date-based puzzle; 随机 selects a seeded challenge. Browser storage is game-scoped and optional. Storage denial or quota failures must not prevent play; progress cannot persist when storage is unavailable. Fullscreen may be unavailable in an iframe and is not required.
