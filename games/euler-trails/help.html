Rules and controls

Cover every edge exactly once. Drag from a pin along connected edges to other pins. Lift to end a stroke. You may revisit a pin, but never reuse an edge. A connected graph requires max(1, odd-degree vertices / 2) strokes; each board shows that proven budget. Not every puzzle is a single-stroke drawing.

Empty taps do not count. A stroke also ends automatically at a pin with no unused exit. Finish all edges without exceeding the budget; undo a bad stroke rather than continuing over budget. U undoes one stroke, H highlights a next move, R restarts. Campaign has 48 boards, plus daily and seeded random modes. Completed records persist, not unfinished ink.

Interface and saves

This guide is not a full translation. Some menus, diagnostic panels and hint prose remain Simplified Chinese. Browser storage is optional and game-scoped; progress cannot persist if storage is blocked or full. Fullscreen is optional and may be unavailable in an iframe.
