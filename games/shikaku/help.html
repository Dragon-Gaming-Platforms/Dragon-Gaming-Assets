Shikaku

Divide the whole grid into non-overlapping rectangles. Each rectangle must contain exactly one clue and its area must equal that clue. Drag between opposite corners to claim a valid rectangle. Tap a 1 clue to claim its single-cell rectangle. Tap an assigned cell to erase its ownership. Line mode records boundary notes; it does not place a rectangle.

Controls

Drag between opposite rectangle corners; tap assigned cells to erase; H hint, Z undo; English help link below game.

  • The original game interface is in Simplified Chinese. Choose one of the five difficulty buttons to begin (left-to-right / top-to-bottom: easiest to hardest). The first tier is recommended for learning.
  • 提示 / H: hint. 撤销 / Z: undo. 暂停 / P: pause. 继续: resume a saved game. 菜单: return to menu. 清空存档: erase this game’s local records (confirmation required).
  • Progress is stored locally in this browser. Reload, then choose 继续 to resume. Saves are not accounts and do not transfer between browsers.
