# Market Lab

A supply-and-demand market with price controls, unit taxes and a monopoly — surplus shaded, deadweight loss measured.

## Features

- Linear supply and demand with live equilibrium, consumer and producer surplus
- Price ceiling and price floor modes where the traded quantity is the short side of the market
- Unit tax mode showing the buyer price, seller price, tax revenue rectangle and deadweight loss triangle
- Single-price monopoly with marginal revenue, restricted output and the welfare comparison against competition

## Controls

Demand and supply intercept sliders, slope, intervention buttons, control size and price line; Space cycles interventions, R resets, S shifts demand

## Implementation

All quantities are analytic: equilibrium at Q = (a−c)/2b, a tax of t shifting quantity by t/2b, and monopoly output at Q = (a−c)/4b. Surpluses are computed as triangle areas from the same two curves that are drawn, so the shaded regions and the numbers cannot drift apart, and total welfare is checked to fall by exactly the deadweight loss.
