Rules and controls

Green frogs move only right; brown frogs move only left. Tap a frog to slide into an adjacent empty pad, or jump over exactly one opposite-color frog into the empty pad beyond. No backward moves, jumps over a same-color frog, double jumps or off-board moves are allowed. Swap the two groups completely.

Glowing frogs have a legal move. A legal move can still strand the puzzle: use Undo or Restart to recover. Tap a frog, or drag it to its legal destination. H gives a hint, U undoes, D demonstrates a solution and R restarts. A demonstration is assistance, not an unassisted record. The campaign includes progressively longer rivers, plus daily and seeded random boards. Records persist when storage is available; reloading restarts a board, not its exact intermediate position.

Interface and storage

This is not a full translation: menus, diagnostics and hint prose remain partly Simplified Chinese. Browser storage is optional and scoped to this game. Records cannot persist if storage is unavailable. Fullscreen is optional and may be unavailable in an iframe.
