Rules and controls

Partition the whole grid into rectangles. Each rectangle contains exactly one printed clue: + means a square, − means wider than tall, and | means taller than wide. No grid intersection may be a common corner of four rectangles. Printed clues cannot be edited.

Every internal edge has three states: unknown, wall, or join (same rectangle). Tap or drag edges to mark them; starting on your existing line erases it. Keys 1/2 choose wall/join and 3 selects unknown. H hints, G adds freely deducible walls, Z undoes, N creates a new puzzle and P/Space pauses. To finish, settle every internal edge, including joins: a rectangle partition alone with undecided edges is not a completed board. Use the on-screen Hint and Undo controls. Five tiers range from 4×4 to 6×6; daily and seed entry are available. Unfinished edge marks and costs can resume.

Interface and saves

This guide is not a full translation. Menus, diagnostics and hint prose remain partly Simplified Chinese. Browser storage is optional and game-scoped; progress cannot persist if storage is blocked or full. Fullscreen is optional and may be unavailable in an iframe.
