    The rules

    A small game.
    An exact challenge.

    1. Blue controls the T → toads on the left and moves first. Amber controls the ← F frogs on the right. Openings have 2, 3, or 4 creatures of each type separated by three spaces.
    2. On your turn move exactly ONE own creature forward: toads only RIGHT, frogs only LEFT. Step one cell into a space, OR jump over one adjacent OPPONENT into the empty cell immediately beyond.
    3. You cannot jump your own creature, make a chain of jumps, capture, move backward, or pass. Jumped creatures remain on the strip.
    4. The player with no legal move on their turn loses, even if the other side still has moves. There is no requirement to swap every creature: this is competitive normal-play Toads and Frogs, distinct from the existing Frog Swap solitaire puzzle.

    Perfect mode preserves a forced win whenever one exists. It cannot force a win from a losing position against perfect defense. Casual chooses a random legal move.
