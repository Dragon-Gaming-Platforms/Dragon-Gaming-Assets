How it works

Rules

  1. Each coloured pair of dots is joined by one continuous path.
  2. Paths may not cross, overlap or pass through another pair’s dot.
  3. Every cell must be covered by exactly one path.

A stroke commits as one move on release. Dragging back over your own stroke shortens it. Hints draw one complete pair path.
