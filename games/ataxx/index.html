    Lasting consequences.

    1. Blue and Amber each start with two discs on opposite corners of a 7×7 board. Blue moves first.
    2. Select one of your discs. Move to any empty square within two king steps (Chebyshev distance). A one-step move clones the disc; a two-step jump vacates the starting square.
    3. After either move, every opposing disc in the eight neighboring squares around the destination becomes yours.
    4. If one player cannot move, that player automatically passes. The game ends when a color disappears, the board fills, or neither player can move. The larger disc count wins; equal counts draw.
    5. Anti-loop house rules: a third occurrence of the same board and player to move, 80 consecutive nonconverting jumps, or 500 completed turns ends in a draw. Cloning or converting resets the quiet-jump counter.
