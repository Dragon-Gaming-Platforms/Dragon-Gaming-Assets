Rules and controls

Choose cells so every row and column matches its clue. A selected cell contributes its column number (starting at 1 on the left) to the row sum and its row number (starting at 1 at the top) to the column sum. For example, selecting columns 2 and 4 gives a row total of 6. Tap again to deselect. Note mode (标记 / N) crosses out cells without adding weight. U: undo; H: hint; R: restart; Escape: close the result card. 战役 = campaign, 每日 = daily, 随机 = random. Campaign and daily boards are precomputed; random generation can take longer.

Interface and saves

The original interface is Simplified Chinese. This English guide is an adaptation, not a full translation. Completed results and campaign unlocks are saved locally when storage is available. Unfinished grids are not saved: reloading starts the current puzzle over. Clearing this game's save does not clear other games. Fullscreen may be unavailable in an embedded player; the game does not require it.
