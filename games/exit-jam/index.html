How it works

Rules

  1. The red target car must slide out through the exit on the right of row three.
  2. Cars and trucks move only along their own direction; they cannot pass through other vehicles.
  3. Each one-cell slide counts as a move. Hints apply the next step on a shortest route and are not refunded.

24 original boards. Hints use breadth-first search for a shortest route; the number on each board is that verified minimum in one-cell slides.
