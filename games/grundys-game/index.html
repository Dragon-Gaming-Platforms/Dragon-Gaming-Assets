    The rules

    A small game.
    An exact challenge.

    1. Blue starts. Choose an opening with one heap of 9, one heap of 15, or two heaps of 25 and 17 counters. All heaps are shared.
    2. On each turn split exactly ONE heap into TWO nonempty heaps of DIFFERENT sizes. For example, 7 may become 1 + 6, 2 + 5, or 3 + 4.
    3. You may not split into equal parts (6 → 3 + 3 is illegal), remove counters, merge heaps, split several heaps at once, or pass. The total number of counters never changes.
    4. Heaps of size 1 or 2 cannot be split. The last player able to split wins; a player with no legal split loses. Multiple starting heaps use the usual disjunctive-sum rule: play in one heap per turn.

    Perfect mode preserves a forced win whenever one exists. It cannot force a win from a losing position against perfect defense. Casual chooses a random legal move.
