Rules and controls

Pair orthogonally adjacent cells into dominoes covering the whole board exactly once. With numbers 1…k, every unordered pair must appear exactly once, including doubles such as (1,1). Thus (1,2) and (2,1) are the same pair. Do not move the printed numbers. This is a partition puzzle, not a competitive dominoes game.

In Pair mode, tap one cell and then its neighbor, or drag across the pair. Tapping an existing domino removes it. In Boundary mode, tap two neighboring cells to note that they cannot be paired. Boundary notes do not count as placed dominoes. Hints use local rules, then contradiction, and may eventually reveal one domino from the unique solution; they are assistance rather than a guarantee that every hint is a local deduction. Three sizes are available: 4×5, 6×7 and 8×9.

This is a single-game extraction from z-biz-game's MIT-licensed Nikoli collection. Other collection games are not included or linked. Size selection, numbered boards and daily play are available. Records/settings persist; the exact unfinished pairing does not survive reloading.

Interface and storage

This is not a full translation: menus, diagnostics and hint prose remain partly Simplified Chinese. Browser storage is optional and scoped to this game. Records cannot persist if storage is unavailable. Fullscreen is optional and may be unavailable in an iframe.
