Turning Turtles

← Play Turning Turtles

Exact rules and edition

  1. Blue starts. A row of shared coins is numbered left to right from 1. H means heads and T means tails; no coin belongs to a player. Choose an 8-, 10-, or 12-coin opening.
  2. Select one HEAD and flip it to tails. In the SAME move, you may optionally flip exactly one coin strictly to its LEFT: heads becomes tails, or tails becomes heads.
  3. Thus a move flips ONE or TWO coins, with the RIGHTMOST flipped coin required to start as heads. You cannot flip only a tail, flip to the right of the chosen head, flip three coins, or pass.
  4. The last player to make a move wins: when every coin is tails, the opponent has no move. Use the Flip head … only action to omit the optional second flip.

Controls

Select a HEAD coin, then Flip only or an optional second coin to its LEFT. Selecting is not a move. Commit a move with a labeled action button, or a highlighted destination on a line board. Cancel selection or Escape clears a selection/suggestion. Tab, Enter and Space work with all native buttons. Left/right arrows move focus along the board. Line boards keep one continuous row and scroll horizontally; a focusable scroll area also supports keyboard scrolling. Contents, numbers, selection and destinations have text/accessible labels; creatures have direction arrows, coins show H/T.

Computer and study tools

Three explicit starting setups use the same rules. Pick a setup, your role and local/computer mode, then press Start new match. Changing menus alone never changes an active match. Blue always starts. Casual is random legal play. Perfect uses: Exact coin-turning invariant: XOR the 1-based positions of all heads. Every legal candidate is evaluated exactly, not by a heuristic, for all supported setups and all reachable positions. It chooses a move to a losing opponent position whenever possible; from a losing position it picks a legal move and relies on a mistake. No computer can force a win from a losing position against perfect defense.

Suggest move selects and labels one exact-policy action without playing it. Study this position → Analyze position reveals the current forced outcome and method, also without playing. Analysis is optional and clears after a move or undo. This is normal-play win/loss analysis, not a full numerical combinatorial-game value or a fastest-win solver.

Undo, pause and saves

Undo restores one move in local play, or your last decision through the computer reply against the computer. It also works after a result and after reload. Before your first decision, the computer opening cannot be undone; start a new match instead. Pause covers the board and stops scheduled computer turns; hidden pages defer computer play.

Only this game's key stores setup/settings and a legal action replay. Reload revalidates every move from the selected initial setup and rebuilds undo history. Selection, analysis and pause state are temporary. Invalid/corrupt replays start fresh, denied storage stays playable during the visit. Clear saved match resets only this game using the selected menus. No account, online opponent, backend, external runtime services or timers.
