Rules and controls

Every person is a Knight (every statement true) or a Knave (every statement false). For every testimony, “the speaker is a Knight” must have the same truth value as their claim. Identify everyone; a complete but inconsistent assignment does not win.

Cards and testimony use English names A, B, C, etc. Each card has ? (unknown), Knave and Knight controls. “Same type” means both Knights or both Knaves. OR is inclusive; IF p THEN q means NOT p OR q, not its converse; IFF means both sides have equal truth values. Parentheses delimit nested claims. Count statements count Knights in the named set. Note marks do not assign identities.

Arrows select a person/statement; 1 assigns Knight, 2 Knave, 0 or Backspace clears, Space cycles identity, . toggles a note. H gives a logical hint, U undoes, R restarts, P pauses. Hint explanations remain partly Chinese, and undo does not refund hints. Choose one of five tiers or the daily puzzle. Each board displays its seed for identification. Unfinished identities and costs resume from the menu; best records prefer fewer hints, then fewer moves, then less time.

Interface and saves

This guide is not a full translation. Some menus, diagnostic panels and hint prose remain Simplified Chinese. Browser storage is optional and game-scoped; progress cannot persist if storage is blocked or full. Fullscreen is optional and may be unavailable in an iframe.
