Rules

This is a cooperative chess-piece puzzle, not a chess match. Move every piece onto one of the marked target squares. Knights jump in an L; rooks slide orthogonally, bishops diagonally and queens both ways. Pieces cannot capture each other. Lit squares block landing and sliding paths. A legal landing toggles the light at that square and its four orthogonal neighbors, changing routes for later moves.

Controls and progress

Tap a piece and then a highlighted dark destination. Z undoes and R restarts the same generated level. After completing a level, use the arrow to generate the next one. Levels grow from 5×5 to 8×8. Reload starts a fresh session: there is no saved progress, timer record, hint system or promise of a unique solution.

Browser storage is optional; records cannot persist if it is blocked or unavailable. All runtime code and assets are local. No account or network service is needed.
