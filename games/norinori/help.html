Rules and controls

The thick region boundaries are given. Shade exactly two cells per region. Every black cell must have exactly one orthogonally adjacent black neighbor, making a 1×2 or 2×1 domino. A domino may cross a region border. Different dominoes cannot share an edge, but may touch diagonally. White cells do not need to be connected.

Select Black or White, then tap cells to place that mark; tap an existing same-color mark to erase it. With the board focused, arrows navigate, Space/Enter marks and E erases. Keys 1/2 select black/white; U undoes, H hints, N creates a new puzzle and P pauses. The board generator runs in a local browser worker; wait for generation to finish. Hint highlights a forced cell and explains its value without filling it; choose the matching pen and mark it yourself. Use Hint for a deduction and Undo to revert a marking action. Region borders cannot be edited. Choose difficulty in the dropdown. Resume is automatic on reload. To share a specific board, use index.html?tier=easy&seed=10 (tiers: easy, standard, tricky, hard). Browser storage remembers progress and costs, not a stored solution.

Interface and saves

This guide is not a full translation. Menus, diagnostics and hint prose remain partly Simplified Chinese. Browser storage is optional and game-scoped; progress cannot persist if storage is blocked or full. Fullscreen is optional and may be unavailable in an iframe.
