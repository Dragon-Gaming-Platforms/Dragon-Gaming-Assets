Rules and controls

Draw borders between cells to partition the whole board into connected blocks. Each block must contain one connected light half and one connected dark half of the same shape and size; rotations and reflections are allowed. A number gives the number of cells of its own color in that block, not the whole block size. Numbers within a block must agree. A block need not contain a number.

Tap an internal dashed segment to add or remove a border. Drag to edit several segments. You do not change the printed shading or fill cells with numbers. A border separates blocks; an open segment joins its cells. The outer frame is fixed. Use arrows to move the cursor and Shift + arrow to toggle its border. H hints, Z/Backspace undoes, R creates a new board, and P/Space pauses. Use the on-screen Undo/Hint controls. Choose one of four tiers from the menu. The seed is displayed; a reproducible board can be opened with index.html#t=sho&s=10 (tiers: sho, chuu, gao, kyoku). Resume restores your borders and costs, not an answer.

Interface and saves

This guide is not a full translation. Menus, diagnostics and hint prose remain partly Simplified Chinese. Browser storage is optional and game-scoped; progress cannot persist if storage is blocked or full. Fullscreen is optional and may be unavailable in an iframe.
