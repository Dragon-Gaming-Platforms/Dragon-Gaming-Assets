Rules and controls

Draw a single closed loop through cell centers, without branching or crossing. Thick lines divide the grid into rooms. The loop must enter and leave every room exactly once (two boundary crossings). In each room it passes through all moons and no suns, or all suns and no moons. Successive rooms along the loop must alternate moon and sun. Tap a cell to cycle between straight lines, corners and exclusion; right-click cycles backward. Arrow keys move the cursor, Enter or Space cycles a shape. Keys 0–6 choose the shapes shown in the legend. H: hint; Z: undo; C: clear the current board; N: new; P: pause. 提示 = hint (points out a cell, does not draw for you); 撤销 = undo; 全清 = clear board; 换一局 = new puzzle. The size menu offers 5×5, 6×6 and 7×7. New puzzles may take a moment to generate.

Interface and saves

The original interface is Simplified Chinese. This English guide is an adaptation, not a full translation. Progress is saved locally in this browser when storage is available. Clearing this game's save does not clear other games. Fullscreen may be unavailable in an embedded player; the game does not require it.
