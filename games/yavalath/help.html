Exact rules and edition

  1. This two-player practice edition uses a side-5 hexagon of 61 cells. Blue starts; alternate placing one own stone on any empty hex.
  2. Four or more consecutive own stones in a straight line along any of the THREE hex-grid axes wins immediately.
  3. Making three consecutive own stones WITHOUT also making four loses immediately. Four takes precedence even if a triple is simultaneously formed in a different direction.
  4. Stones never move or capture. No passing. This practice edition deliberately omits the usual pie/swap opening option; colors stay fixed. A full board with no decisive line is a draw. This is not the three-player variant.

Controls and accessibility

Tap an empty hex to place. Native buttons support Tab, Enter and Space. Arrow keys move focus to a nearby point in the chosen direction. Escape or Cancel selection clears a selection or suggestion. Board labels announce numbered hexes with axial q/r coordinates, ring points with a center label, or square-board algebraic coordinates. Piece labels and distinct round/rounded-square shapes identify Blue and Amber; colors alone are not required.

Computer play

Select local two-player or computer play, your color and a level, then press Start new match. Changing menus alone does not alter the active match. Blue always starts. Casual chooses a random legal action. Tactical evaluates all legal moves and all immediate opponent replies using a two-ply minimax policy, with mobility/centrality/clustering heuristics beyond that horizon. It checks immediate wins/losses and uses the same documented draw rules, but it is NOT expert, optimal, or a complete solver. Dodo mobility is deliberately scored in reverse because immobility wins. Suggest turn shows that policy without playing.

Undo, pause and saves

Undo restores one move in local play, or your last decision through the computer reply against the computer. It works after results and reload. Before your first decision the computer opening cannot be undone; start a new match instead. Pause covers the board and cancels scheduled computer turns. Hidden pages defer computer play.

Only this game's browser key stores settings and a legal action replay. Reload revalidates each action from the initial setup and rebuilds undo and repetition history. Corrupt/illegal replays start fresh. Denied storage remains playable during the visit. Clear saved match affects only this game and starts fresh using the selected menus. Selection and pause are temporary. No accounts, online opponents, external runtime services, backend or timers.
