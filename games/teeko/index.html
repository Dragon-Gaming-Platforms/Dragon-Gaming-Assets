    1. Blue and Amber each have four stones on a 5×5 grid. Blue starts. On the first eight turns, alternate placing one stone on any empty cell.
    2. After all eight stones are placed, move one of your stones one square horizontally, vertically or diagonally to an empty cell. No jumping, capturing or passing.
    3. Win immediately whenever your four stones form a straight horizontal, vertical or diagonal line, or the four corners of a filled 2×2 square. A win may happen during placement.
    4. This is the basic small-square rule, not advanced variants that allow larger squares. A player with no legal move loses.
    5. Anti-loop house rules: the same board and player to move appearing three times, or reaching 200 turns without a winner, is a draw.

    Teeko rules

    1. Blue and Amber each have four stones on a 5×5 grid. Blue starts. On the first eight turns, alternate placing one stone on any empty cell.
    2. After all eight stones are placed, move one of your stones one square horizontally, vertically or diagonally to an empty cell. No jumping, capturing or passing.

    Each action is one turn. Placement games use a single marked-cell tap; movement/removal games use a source and destination. Full controls, computer limits and saving details are in the guide.
