Exact edition rules

  1. The board is 8×8. Blue occupies the TOP two rows and moves first. Amber occupies the BOTTOM two rows. Blue advances downward; Amber upward.
  2. Move one knight in a forward L: two rows forward and one column sideways, OR one row forward and two columns sideways. Backward and purely sideways moves are forbidden.
  3. Knights jump over intervening pieces. A destination may be empty or occupied by an enemy knight, which is captured. You cannot land on your own knight.
  4. Reach any square on the opposite back rank to win immediately. You also win if the opponent has no legal move. There is no check, promotion, castling or passing.

Controls

Select your own knight, then tap a marked landing square. Captures are automatic. Native buttons support Tab, Enter and Space; arrows navigate the board without horizontal row wrapping. Escape or Cancel selection clears a selection or suggestion. Cell labels announce coordinates, contents and legal destinations. Knights jump over all intervening pieces. Only the landing square matters.

Local and computer play

Select local two-player or computer play, your role and a level, then press Start new match. Menu changes alone do not alter the active match. The first listed role always starts. Casual picks a random legal action. Tactical: Bounded two-ply heuristic with immediate terminal checks; not expert or optimal play. NoGo scores own minus opponent legal mobility after every candidate. Other games inspect every opponent reply for terminal results and sample at most 1,600 nonterminal reply heuristics in total, using forward progress/material in Knightthrough and unmixed five-cell windows in Order and Chaos. Traffic Lights and Notakto use terminal avoidance only, not deep solvers. Suggest turn displays that same policy without playing it.

Undo, pause and local saving

Undo restores one move in local play, or your last decision through the computer reply in computer play. Undo works after results and reload. A computer opening before your first decision cannot be undone; start a new match instead. Pause covers the board and stops scheduled computer play. Hidden pages defer computer turns.

Only this game's key stores settings plus a legal action replay. Every action, including the selected X/O mark, is revalidated from the initial board on reload, rebuilding undo history. Corrupt or illegal saves start fresh. Denied storage remains playable for this visit. Clear saved match affects only this game and starts fresh with selected settings. No accounts, online opponents, external services or timers.
