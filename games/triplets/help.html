Rules and controls

Regions are already outlined; each contains exactly three cells. Fill all cells with square ■, circle ● or triangle ▲. In each region, its three symbols must be either all equal or all different. Orthogonally adjacent cells in different regions must differ. There is no row/column balance, connectivity or loop rule. Given symbols cannot be changed.

Select a symbol and tap a cell. Keys 1/2/3 choose square/circle/triangle; arrows move the cursor and Enter/Space writes. Delete erases, E cycles tools, H gives a deduction, Z undoes, P pauses the clock. The note tool edits symbol candidates, not final placements; finish with actual symbols. Choose board size, use New to generate, or enter a seed. The seed, notes, moves and elapsed time resume after reload. There is no campaign or daily mode.

Interface and saves

This guide is not a full translation. Some menus, diagnostic panels and hint prose remain Simplified Chinese. Browser storage is optional and game-scoped; progress cannot persist if storage is blocked or full. Fullscreen is optional and may be unavailable in an iframe.
