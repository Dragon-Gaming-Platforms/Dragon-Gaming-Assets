Rules and controls

Shade cells to form solid, axis-aligned square regions. Different black squares may touch at corners, but not along an edge. Numbered and question-mark cells stay white. A number is the sum of the full areas of the black squares touching it orthogonally, not merely the number of adjacent black cells. A question mark needs at least one adjacent black cell. All white cells must be connected through edges. Single black cells count as squares; a zero clue permits no adjacent black region. Tap a cell to cycle unknown → black → white → unknown. Right-click toggles black. Arrow keys move focus; Enter or Space cycles a cell. 提示一步 = hint; 检查 = check; 重开本题 / R = restart this puzzle; 换一局 = next puzzle. Choose a tier above the board. Larger generated puzzles can take a moment.

Interface and saves

The original interface is Simplified Chinese. This guide is not a full translation. Saves are local to this browser when storage is available. Each game has its own namespaced keys; storage failures should not prevent play. Fullscreen may be unavailable in an iframe and is not required.
