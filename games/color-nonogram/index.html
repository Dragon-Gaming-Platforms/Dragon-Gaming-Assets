How it works

Rules

  1. Each row and column lists its runs of colour in order.
  2. Two runs of the same colour in a line need at least one gap; different colours may touch.
  3. Paint the cells to match every clue. Crosses mark cells you know are empty.

Checking uses the clues, not the stored picture. Each puzzle is the only picture that fits its clues.
