How it works

Rules

  1. Cars are 2 or 3 cells long and slide only along their direction.
  2. Move the red car X through the exit on the right side of its row.
  3. Blocked cars cannot move. Fewer steps is better; the best known count is shown for each puzzle.

24 original 6×6 puzzles in three tiers, by the optimal step count found by a breadth-first solver (not human-rated difficulty).
