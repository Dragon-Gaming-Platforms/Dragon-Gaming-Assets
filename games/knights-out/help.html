Rules

Move your knight in an L (two squares one way, one the other), landing only on a lit, unoccupied square. Landing turns that light off. Shadow knights copy the same jump; mirror knights copy it with left/right reversed. Echoes may land on dark squares and turn them on. An echo blocked by an edge, gap or another knight stays put. Turn every square dark to win.

Controls and progress

Tap your knight, then a highlighted destination. Hover, or press and hold on touch, to preview echoes. Z undoes, R restarts, H hints and Esc opens levels. There are 36 campaign levels and generated endless boards. Hints may explain how far to undo a dead end. Progress and sound settings persist, but exact intermediate positions do not.

Browser storage is optional; records cannot persist if it is blocked or unavailable. All runtime code and assets are local. No account or network service is needed.
