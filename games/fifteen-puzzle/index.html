How it works

Rules

  1. Tiles 1 to N²−1 start shuffled around one blank space.
  2. Only a tile next to the blank may slide into it.
  3. Put the tiles in order, with the blank in the bottom-right corner, to solve.

Hints suggest a move that reduces total distance from home. This is a helpful nudge, not a guaranteed-optimal route.
