Rules and controls

Move every ball into a different H hole. A ball starting with k must first move k cells in one straight orthogonal stroke, then k−1, then k−2, and so on. A ball stops permanently when it lands in a hole; it need not use every possible stroke. Routes cannot cross or overlap themselves or other routes, pass through another ball, or pass through a hole (ending in it is allowed). A stroke may cross water, but cannot end in water or outside the board. Select a ball and tap the endpoint of each full stroke. Arrow keys make a full stroke in the chosen direction; Space selects another ball. Z or Backspace: undo; H: logical hint; R: restart this puzzle. 提示 = hint; 撤销 = undo; 重开 = restart; 换一局 = new puzzle. Select a tier from the opening menu. Progress can be resumed from that menu. Larger generated boards may take longer.

Interface and saves

The original interface is Simplified Chinese. This guide is not a full translation. Saves are local to this browser when storage is available. Each game has its own namespaced keys; storage failures should not prevent play. Fullscreen may be unavailable in an iframe and is not required.
