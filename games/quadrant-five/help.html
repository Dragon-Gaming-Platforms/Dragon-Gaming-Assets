Quadrant Five

← Play Quadrant Five

Exact rules

  1. The 6×6 board has four 3×3 quadrants. Blue starts. First place one stone on any empty cell.
  2. Then rotate exactly one quadrant 90° clockwise or counterclockwise using a rotation button. You may rotate a different quadrant from the one where you placed. Even an empty or unchanged-looking quadrant is a legal twist.
  3. Five or more consecutive stones in a horizontal, vertical or diagonal straight line wins. If placement alone makes five, the game ends immediately without a twist.
  4. After a twist, an alignment wins for its owner, even when made by the opponent. If BOTH colors have five at that point, the game is a draw. A full board without either alignment is a draw after the required twist.
  5. No pie/swap rule or twist-skipping option is used. This independent implementation is not affiliated with any publisher of place-and-rotate board games.

Controls and computer play

Tap an empty cell to place, then choose one of the eight labeled quadrant rotation buttons. Cancel turn during the twist stage restores the whole turn to its start. Escape or Cancel selection clears a suggestion before placing.

Native cell buttons support Tab, Enter and Space; arrow keys navigate without wrapping rows. Rotation buttons support Tab/Enter/Space. Cell labels announce coordinates, contents and legal destinations. Blue stones are round/B-marked, Amber stones rounded squares/A-marked.

Choose local two-player or computer play, your color and a computer level, then press Start new match. Menus alone do not change the active match. Blue always starts. Casual chooses a random legal complete turn. Tactical scores at most 512 evenly sampled complete turns using a one-ply game-specific space/center heuristic (plus immediate-threat guards in Squava/Neutron). It is not deep, optimal or expert play. Suggest turn shows that same policy without making the move.

Undo, pause and saving

Undo restores the current unfinished turn, or one full turn in local play. Against the computer it restores your previous decision through the computer’s reply. Undo works after results and reload. Before your first decision, the computer’s opening cannot be undone; use Start new match. Pause covers the board and cancels scheduled computer play; hidden pages defer computer moves.

Only this game’s key stores settings plus a legal action replay, including an unfinished placement/twist turn. Every saved action is revalidated from the original setup on reload, rebuilding undo history. Invalid/corrupt saves start fresh; denied storage remains playable. Clear saved match affects only this game and starts fresh with selected settings. No accounts, online opponents, external runtime services, timers or rankings.
