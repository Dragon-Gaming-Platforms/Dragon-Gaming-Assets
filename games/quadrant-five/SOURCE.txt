Original rules/computer code; first-party board controller and CSS adapted from
this repository's previous collections. No upstream mirror, borrowed external
code, art, sounds, fonts or runtime packages. Established mechanics are not
claimed as new inventions; no affiliation with any publisher is implied.
Exact edition, board sizes, house rules and AI limits are documented in-game.
Rules are documented fully in help.html.

Computer: random legal complete turns or a bounded 512-candidate one-ply
heuristic, not expert/optimal search. Saved action replays are revalidated.
Everything needed lives in this folder; no build step, accounts, backend,
network dependencies or service worker.
