Rules

You place red vertical dominoes on an 8×8 board; the computer places blue horizontal dominoes. Each domino occupies two empty orthogonally adjacent cells. Click the upper square of your intended vertical domino. You win when the computer has no horizontal placement; you lose when you have no vertical placement. Passing and overlapping placements are not allowed.

Controls and progress

Tap or click the upper square to place a red domino. The computer searches two plies and responds automatically; this older engine can briefly pause the page while thinking. The status line announces the result and Restart clears the board. This edition is single-player versus the computer, with no undo, hint, score storage or adjustable board size.

Browser storage is optional; records cannot persist if it is blocked or unavailable. All runtime code and assets are local. No account or network service is needed.
