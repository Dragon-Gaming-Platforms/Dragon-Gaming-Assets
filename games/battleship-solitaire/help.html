Rules and controls

Place every ship in the fleet list. Each row and column must contain exactly its stated number of ship cells. Ships are straight; different ships cannot touch, even at a corner. Tap a ship to remove it. A tap on empty water places the shortest remaining ship; drag to specify a longer one. To finish, mark every remaining cell as water, not just the ships. Turn on the Water pen button and tap these cells (works on touchscreens); turn it off to place ships again. Shift-click is an alternative on desktop. Water is accepted only when both intersecting totals are already satisfied. Use Undo (撤销), Hint (提示), Restart (重开) and Share (分享题面). U: undo; H: hint; R: restart. These are deduction puzzles, not battles against an opponent.

Interface and saves

The original interface is Simplified Chinese. This English guide is an adaptation, not a full translation. Progress is saved locally in this browser when storage is available. Clearing this game's save does not clear other games. Fullscreen may be unavailable in an embedded player; the game does not require it.
