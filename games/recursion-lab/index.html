Recursion Lab

Three classic recursions, made visible: the Tower of Hanoi, the Fibonacci call tree, and factorial. Each one solves a big problem by calling itself on smaller ones.

Hanoi: move the whole tower one disk at a time, never placing a larger disk on a smaller one. Fibonacci: the call tree — watch it explode exponentially. Factorial: the chain of shrinking calls.

Hanoi needs exactly 2ⁿ − 1 moves — no fewer will ever do. The recursion proves it: move n−1 disks, move the largest, move n−1 again.
