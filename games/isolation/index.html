    Lasting consequences.

    1. This is a 7×7 edition of the isolation game. Each player has one runner; Blue moves first.
    2. First, move your runner one square in any of the eight directions to an intact, empty floor cell. You cannot enter the other runner’s square or a removed square.
    3. Then remove any empty floor cell anywhere on the board. You may remove the square you just left. Occupied squares cannot be removed.
    4. Removed floor never comes back. After the removal, the other player takes a turn. A player who cannot move their runner loses.
