Exact rules

  1. Blue and Amber alternate placing one of their stones on an empty cell of a 5×5 board. Blue starts. Stones do not move or capture.
  2. Make four consecutive stones of your color in a horizontal, vertical or diagonal straight line to win.
  3. If your placement makes three consecutive stones without also making a four, you LOSE immediately. Four takes precedence even when the same placement also makes a triple in another direction.
  4. Lines cannot wrap around an edge. No pie/swap rule is used in this edition. A full board with neither a winning four nor a losing triple is a draw.

Controls and computer play

Tap a marked empty cell to place a stone. Escape or Cancel selection clears a suggestion without playing it.

Native cell buttons support Tab, Enter and Space; arrow keys navigate without wrapping rows. Cell labels announce coordinates, contents and legal destinations. Blue stones are round/B-marked, Amber stones rounded squares/A-marked.

Choose local two-player or computer play, your color and a computer level, then press Start new match. Menus alone do not change the active match. Blue always starts. Casual chooses a random legal complete turn. Tactical scores at most 512 evenly sampled complete turns using a one-ply game-specific space/center heuristic (plus immediate-threat guards in Squava/Neutron). It is not deep, optimal or expert play. Suggest turn shows that same policy without making the move.

Undo, pause and saving

Undo restores the current unfinished turn, or one full turn in local play. Against the computer it restores your previous decision through the computer’s reply. Undo works after results and reload. Before your first decision, the computer’s opening cannot be undone; use Start new match. Pause covers the board and cancels scheduled computer play; hidden pages defer computer moves.

Only this game’s key stores settings plus a legal action replay, from the opening through the result. Every saved action is revalidated from the original setup on reload, rebuilding undo history. Invalid/corrupt saves start fresh; denied storage remains playable. Clear saved match affects only this game and starts fresh with selected settings. No accounts, online opponents, external runtime services, timers or rankings.
