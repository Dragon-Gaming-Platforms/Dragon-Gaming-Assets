Rules and controls

Move matches to make the equation true. A move removes one lit, unlocked segment and adds one unlit, unlocked segment. Drag a match to its new slot. The two equals-sign matches are locked. Invalid drops snap back without costing a move.

Digits must retain valid seven-segment forms, and multi-digit numbers cannot start with zero. Operators are +, − and ×. Multiplication takes priority; operators of equal priority are evaluated left to right. Intermediate negative results are not accepted. The panel’s minimum is certified by exhaustive search. H / 提示 identifies an optimal next move; using hints prevents a perfect rating. U / 撤销 undoes; R / 重来 restarts; Escape dismisses results. Completed records, not unfinished equations, are saved.

Interface and saves

The original interface is Simplified Chinese; this guide is not a full translation. Campaign / 战役 selects regular puzzles; 每日 selects a date-based puzzle; 随机 selects a seeded challenge. Browser storage is game-scoped and optional. Storage denial or quota failures must not prevent play; progress cannot persist when storage is unavailable. Fullscreen may be unavailable in an iframe and is not required.
