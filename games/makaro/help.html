Makaro

← Play Makaro

Exact rules

  1. Fill every white cell in a room of N cells with the numbers 1 through N, each used exactly once. Bold borders separate rooms; black cells are not part of any room.
  2. Two numbered cells sharing an edge must have different numbers, including across room borders. Diagonal equality is allowed.
  3. An arrow on a black cell must point to the STRICTLY LARGEST number among all white cells immediately above, below, left or right of that arrow cell. Ignore other black cells and positions off the board.
  4. Arrow cells and plain black cells remain unfilled. Gold-outlined numbers are fixed clues. These puzzles have no extra row or column uniqueness rule.

Pack and daily selection

24 original generated puzzles: eight each on 4×4, 5×5 and 6×6 boards. Every stored answer satisfies the full rules, and an offline Z3 solver proved that no second solution exists for each puzzle. No solver package or network service is loaded by the game. Board sizes are not human-rated difficulty tiers. The UTC daily button chooses day-number modulo 24 from this same local pack; it repeats every 24 days, not a limitless generator.

Editing and notes

For number puzzles, select a cell then use the keypad or keyboard digits. For Nondango, select Black, White or Erase first, then tap circles to paint. Gold-outlined givens and blocked/hole cells cannot change. 0 means a confirmed empty cell in Sukoro/Nanro or a white circle in Nondango, never a numbered zero. Renban and Makaro require a positive digit in every white cell. Delete/Backspace erases to undecided.

Number puzzles have pencil notes: toggle Notes or press N, then enter digits into an undecided cell to toggle candidates. Notes do not fill the cell or participate in Check. Erase clears both the value and notes. Arrow keys navigate within rows/columns, skipping fixed cells without wrapping; Tab, Enter and Space work with native controls. Z undoes, Y redoes, H reveals an answer. Undo/redo work after completion and after reload; opening another puzzle or Restart replaces undo history. Selection, note-mode and pause are temporary.

Check, hints and completion

Check reports direct rule contradictions in the current entries, allowing undecided cells as potential future connections. A clean partial check is NOT proof that every entry is correct or that the partial board is solvable. Completion requires every editable cell to be decided and all full rules to pass, not merely agreement with the stored answer.

Hint reveals one answer cell, correcting an entered wrong value before filling an undecided cell. It is not a logical-deduction explanation; the attempt is marked assisted. Hint usage is not refunded by undo. Best records prioritize fewer answer reveals, then fewer edits, then active seconds. Notes and undo/redo count as edits. Solved records remain when you undo or reopen a puzzle.

Pause and browser saves

The active timer stops during Pause, while the page is hidden and after completion. Pause covers the board and disables edits/check/hints. Your current board, notes, up to 100 undo/redo frames, counters and best records are saved only under this game's browser key. Frames, known puzzle IDs, fixed clues and value ranges are validated on reload; corrupt data starts fresh or drops invalid history. Denied storage stays playable. Clear local progress clears only this game's records and restarts puzzle 1. Switching puzzles saves one active board, not an unfinished board per puzzle.
