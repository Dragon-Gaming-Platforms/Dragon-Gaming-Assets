Fox and Hounds

← Play Fox and Hounds

Rules

The fox starts at the bottom and tries to reach the top row. Four hounds try to block every legal fox move. All animals move one diagonal square onto an empty dark square; the fox can move up or down, but hounds can only move toward the bottom. There are no captures. The fox also wins if the hounds cannot move.

Controls and progress

Click or tap an animal and then a dark destination. The default is human fox versus biased-minimax hounds. Uncheck Enable Hounds for local two-player play, or enable both sides for a simulation. Each side can use random play or minimax with integer depth 1–4 and Pure, Biased or Average evaluation. New board restarts; score Reset also clears session wins. Wins stop play unless Automatically restart after wins is enabled. Pause all pauses automatic AI; Advance one move requests one computer move. The optional Allow invalid moves switch is for experiments and deliberately disables normal legality checks. Nothing persists after reload.

All runtime assets are local and no account or server API is needed. Optional browser storage may be unavailable in private browsing or restricted iframes; the games remain playable without it.
