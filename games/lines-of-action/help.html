Rules

Connect all of your pieces into one group, including diagonal neighbors. A piece moves along a row, column or diagonal exactly as many squares as there are pieces of either color on that entire line. You may jump your own pieces, never enemy pieces. You may capture an enemy by landing on it, but cannot land on your own piece. The mover wins if both groups become connected at once.

Controls and progress

Tap a piece and then a legal destination, or drag it. New game offers computer, two-player and AI-vs-AI modes, color, difficulty and the Scrambled Eggs variant. Undo, hints, move-history review and illustrated rules are included. English and Portuguese are supported. The current game and settings persist when storage is available.

Browser storage is optional; records cannot persist if it is blocked or unavailable. All runtime code and assets are local. No account or network service is needed.
