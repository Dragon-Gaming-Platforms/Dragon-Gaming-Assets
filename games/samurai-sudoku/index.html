How it works

Rules

  1. Five 9×9 grids overlap at their corners. Fill every open cell of all five grids.
  2. Each row, column and 3×3 box of each grid, read within that grid, holds the digits 1–9 once.
  3. Red cells conflict with another entry. Bold digits are fixed clues and cannot be changed.

24 original puzzles in three tiers, by number of starting clues (not human-rated difficulty). Each has one solution across all five grids.
