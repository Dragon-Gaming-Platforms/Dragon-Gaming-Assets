FIELD GUIDE

3 regions
  • Place reinforcements on your own territories. You receive at least three each turn.
  • Then select a friendly territory with 2+ armies and an adjacent target to attack.
  • Dice are rolled automatically; ties favor the defender. Captures move armies forward.
  • Control 9 territories to win. Full control of a region grants a bonus next turn.
