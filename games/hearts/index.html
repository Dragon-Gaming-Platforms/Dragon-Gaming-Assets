How to Play Hearts ✕

Object of the Game

Hearts is a trick-taking game for four players. The goal is to have the lowest score. Play continues until a player reaches 100 points.

Scoring

  • Each ♥ heart is worth 1 point.
  • The ♠ Queen of Spades is worth 13 points.
  • Shooting the Moon: take all hearts and the Queen of Spades to give every other player 26 points instead.

How to Play

  • Each round begins by passing 3 cards (left, right, across, then no pass).
  • The player holding the 2 of Clubs leads the first trick.
  • You must follow the led suit if you can.
  • Hearts cannot be led until they are "broken" (played on another suit), unless you have only hearts left.
  • No hearts or the Queen of Spades may be played on the first trick.
