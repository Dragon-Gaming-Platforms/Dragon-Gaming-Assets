← Play Trees and Tents

Rules

Place one tent for each tree in an orthogonally adjacent cell. Every tree must be paired to a distinct tent, and every tent to a distinct tree. Tents may not touch, even diagonally. Numbers outside the board give the exact tent count in each row and column. Trees themselves do not count as tents. A perfect one-to-one pairing is required, not just the right total number of tents.

Controls and progress

Use the Tent or Grass tool, then tap a cell; tap the same mark with the same tool to erase it. Trees cannot be changed. Grass is an optional note, not required for a win. Ten camps grow from 5×5 to 9×9 and unlock in order. There are three hints per camp; they use the authored solution, not a general deduction solver. Reset restarts the current camp. The win card lets you replay or continue. English/Chinese language selection and completed camps persist when storage is available; intermediate boards, time and sound toggles do not.

All runtime assets are local and no account or server API is needed. Optional browser storage may be unavailable in private browsing or restricted iframes; the games remain playable without it.
