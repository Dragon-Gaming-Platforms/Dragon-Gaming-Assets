Abalone

Push six out to win. Two players, one screen.

Rules

Push six out

  1. Black moves first. Each turn, select one, two or three of your marbles in a straight, touching line.
  2. Move them one cell: along the line (inline), or sideways into empty cells (broadside). A single marble moves into an empty cell only.
  3. An inline move may push opposing marbles that sit directly ahead, but only when you have more marbles in the line than they do. Pushed marbles that leave the board count as pushed out.
  4. Push six opposing marbles out to win. If 100 moves pass without a marble being pushed out, the game is a draw.

Click a marble to select it. Highlighted cells are legal destinations. Clicking a non-highlighted cell next to the selection makes no move.
