Exact rules

  1. This edition plays Snort on the orthogonal-neighbor graph of a 7×7 square grid. Blue and Amber alternate placing one stone on an empty cell; Blue starts.
  2. You may not place a stone directly above, below, left or right of an opponent’s stone. Diagonal contact with either color is allowed.
  3. Orthogonally adjacent stones of your OWN color are allowed. Placed stones never move or disappear.
  4. A player with no legal placement loses. There is no passing, scoring or requirement to connect all your stones.

Controls and computer levels

For placement games tap/click a marked empty cell. For movement games select your piece, then a marked destination. For Kayles select a standing pin, then the same pin for a single removal or its standing horizontal neighbor for a pair. Escape or Cancel selection clears a selection/suggestion.

Native cell buttons support Tab, Enter and Space. Arrow keys navigate without wrapping rows. Cell labels announce coordinates, contents and legal destinations. Blue pieces are round/B-marked, Amber pieces rounded squares/A-marked. Shared X marks and pins are neutral, not either player’s pieces.

Choose local two-player or a computer opponent, your color and the computer level, then press Start new match to apply the settings. Menus alone do not change the active match. Blue always moves first. Casual selects a random legal action. Tactical is described below for this game; Suggest turn uses that same policy without playing the action.

Undo, pause and replay-validated saving

Undo takes back one turn in local play. Against the computer it returns to your previous decision, taking back through the computer reply. Undo works after results and after reloading. The computer opening cannot be taken back before your first decision; use a new match instead. Pause covers the board and stops scheduled computer actions; computer actions are deferred while the page is hidden.

The match is saved only under this game’s local-storage key as settings plus a legal action replay. Every saved action is revalidated from the original setup on reload, rebuilding undo history. Invalid/corrupt saves start fresh; denied storage remains playable without persistence. Clear saved match affects only this game and starts a fresh match using the selected settings. No accounts, online opponents, network dependencies, timers, rankings or external services.

This game’s Tactical policy

Tactical uses a one-ply legal-space heuristic, not an expert search.
