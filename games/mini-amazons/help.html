Mini Amazons

← Play Mini Amazons

Exact rules

  1. This compact variant uses a 6×6 board and two amazons per side, not the standard 10×10/four-amazon setup. Blue moves first.
  2. Move one of your amazons any number of empty squares along a rank, file or diagonal, like a chess queen. You cannot jump over pieces or arrows.
  3. From its new position, fire one arrow any number of empty squares along a rank, file or diagonal. You may shoot back to or through the square just vacated. The new amazon position blocks the opposite ray as usual.
  4. The arrow permanently blocks its landing cell. You cannot shoot onto or through an occupied or blocked cell. Finish both the move and the shot before the other player’s turn.
  5. A player with no legal amazon move loses. Every possible queen move leaves its old square available for a legal return shot.

Controls and match settings

Tap/click your piece, then a marked destination. For Isolation or Mini Amazons, finish by tapping a legal removal/arrow square. Escape or Cancel selection clears a selection; during an unfinished two-part turn it restores the whole turn to its start. Click a selected piece again to deselect it.

Native cell buttons support Tab, Enter and Space. Arrow keys navigate the board without wrapping rows. Cell labels announce coordinates, contents and legal destinations. Blue pieces are round and marked B; Amber pieces are rounded squares and marked A, so color is not the only signal.

Choose local two-player or a computer opponent, your color and a computer level in New match settings; press Start new match to apply them. Changing the menus alone does not affect the active match. A new match replaces the old saved match. Blue always starts. Casual chooses a random legal complete turn. Tactical evaluates at most 512 evenly sampled complete turns with a one-ply material/mobility/space heuristic; it is not an expert or a guaranteed winning opponent. Suggest turn uses the same bounded tactical player and does not make the move.

Undo, pause and local saving

Undo takes back an unfinished turn, or a full turn in local play. Against the computer it takes back through the computer’s reply to your last decision. The computer’s opening move cannot be taken back before you have made a decision; use Start new match instead. Undo also works after a result. Pause stops computer scheduling and covers the board. Computer moves are deferred while the page is hidden.

The active match is saved under this game’s own local-storage key as settings plus a legal action replay, including an unfinished move/removal or move/arrow turn. On reload, every action is revalidated from the original setup, and undo history is rebuilt. Invalid/corrupt saves start fresh. Denied storage remains playable without persistence. Clear saved match affects only this game and starts a new match with the selected settings. There are no accounts, network opponents, timers, rankings or external services.
