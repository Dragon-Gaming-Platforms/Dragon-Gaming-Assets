# Media provenance

Designed by Charles. Produced by Cédric Bernadac. Code and procedural geometry by Nilo (Codex), MIT, see LICENSE.

No music or sound recordings have been delivered; do not attribute any to Suno or ElevenLabs. The sounds are original oscillator synthesis.

The sprite sheets in `assets/sprites/` (the three heroes, the wild monsters, the Old Glasswing boss and its form) are original art made for Glasswing in November 2026: Blender 4.5 scenes built by scripts that Kimi K3.1 Pro and Claude agents wrote under the direction of Charles and Cédric Bernadac, rendered and packed into WebP sheets. No third-party models, textures or references were included. The producer, Cédric Bernadac, released them under MIT, like the game, for LOB7 edition 0.4.0. The Blender sources stay outside this package, because LOB7 rejects .py and .blend files. The other bosses and effects are drawn procedurally by the game's code (MIT).

`assets/store/cover-0.3.3.png` (formerly `assets/cover-combat.png`) is an original 1280 × 720 gameplay capture of the default procedural renderer, MIT. It shows frame 9859 of `tests/replays/normal-spark.json` in the Nest, reproduced from the v0.3.2 simulation. The image is cropped around the aerial boss fight; no characters, effects or scenery were added. The HUD is omitted. It was the author image of LOB7 edition 0.3.3; edition 0.4.0 uses `assets/store/cover.webp` instead. It was later re-encoded losslessly (its alpha was fully opaque, so RGBA became RGB with the same pixels) to keep the base package under 3 MB.

`assets/store/` holds the LOB7 cover and gallery for edition 0.4.0: 1600 × 900 WebP captures of the game's own renderer, taken by headless Chrome during the November 2026 integration tests (title screen, the Unborn Chrysalis and Tallowgrave fights, and the Antiphon mask in the Hall of Masks). MIT, like the game; the sprites they show are MIT as stated above. Nothing was painted over them. They have their own 1 MB budget in `tests/package.test.mjs`, outside the 3 MB game package.

Future original Blender models supplied by Kimi K3.1 under the direction of Charles and Cédric Bernadac are intended for CC0-1.0. Confirm that dedication for each actual delivery before adding it. Store sources on dev-assets, not main.

Future Suno music and ElevenLabs recordings require the generation date, account/license tier, asset identifier, and applicable distribution terms here before publication. No rights are asserted over undelivered media. Silent manifest entries are intentional.

Fredoka and Nunito are bundled under SIL OFL 1.1; full notices are in assets/fonts/.

The three files in assets/test/ are original programmatically generated GLB test fixtures, MIT, generated by tools/make-fixtures.py, preserved on the dev-assets branch. Python source is excluded from the LOB7 static package. They are engineering fixtures, not Kimi art, and are only activated by the alternate test manifest. The default game uses procedural characters and forest. Original oscillator sound placeholders are part of the MIT code.
