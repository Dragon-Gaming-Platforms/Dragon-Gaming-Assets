Superposition makes the pattern

Each point emitter sends out circular waves. The canvas adds their signed amplitudes, revealing constructive crests and destructive nodes. Drag emitters, change wavelength or phase, and compare arrangements. This is a qualitative model, not a fluid or acoustics solver.
