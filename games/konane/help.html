Rules

This edition uses the native free-opening variant of Kōnane: Black removes any one of its own stones, then White removes one stone immediately orthogonally adjacent to that vacancy. Starting with Black, move by jumping orthogonally over an opposing stone into an empty square, removing the jumped stone. You may make several consecutive captures in one straight direction, stopping at any valid landing. No diagonal jumps, turns within a move, ordinary noncapturing moves or passes. The player with no capture on their turn loses.

Controls and progress

Computer mode starts automatically: the computer is Black and you are White. Remove a White stone beside its opening vacancy, then select one of your stones and a highlighted destination. The computer uses a shallow mobility heuristic, not a championship engine. Two players switches to local hot-seat play; Restart begins a fresh game in the same mode. The board scales for touch, and the status announces turns and the winner. No undo, hints, scores or saved positions.

All runtime assets are local and no account or server API is needed. Optional browser storage may be unavailable in private browsing or restricted iframes; the games remain playable without it.

License and source

Original game by Kyle Harrity, MIT. Hosting adaptations fix scaled pointer input, annotation masks, bounds and terminal locking; they add responsive sizing, restart and a local two-player mode while retaining native capture and computer-move logic.
