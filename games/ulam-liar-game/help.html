Rules and controls

A fixed secret lies in 1..n. The opponent knows it and may lie at most k times. Select any subset of cells (tap individually or brush across them), then press Ask / 问出去 to ask whether the secret is in that subset. Even the empty/full subset is legal. You have at most q questions. A candidate is eliminated once more than k answers contradict it.

When only one candidate remains or questions run out, the board enters accusation mode: tap the number you believe is the secret. Do not keep trying to select a subset then. You can win by a lucky guess with multiple candidates left, but that is not a guaranteed solution. The English panel reports the phase, answer and remaining candidates. The volume lower bound is not always sufficient; the exact DP minimum is listed separately. U undoes a question, H shows a suggested subset, R restarts; Enter asks. Campaign, daily and seeded random modes are available. Campaign/daily boards carry exact policies; generated/custom boards may offer heuristic hints rather than a certified strategy. Completed records persist locally; unfinished question history resumes in the same browser tab.

Interface and saves

This guide is not a full translation. Some menus, diagnostic panels and hint prose remain Simplified Chinese. Browser storage is optional and game-scoped; progress cannot persist if storage is blocked or full. Fullscreen is optional and may be unavailable in an iframe.
