Cave rules

  1. Numbered cells stay clear. A number counts visible clear cells horizontally and vertically, including the numbered cell once. A shaded cell blocks the view.
  2. All clear cave cells form one nonempty orthogonally connected area.
  3. Every shaded component must touch the outer board edge through shaded cells sharing edges. Enclosed shaded islands are forbidden.
  4. There is no ban on solid 2×2 blocks or on adjacent shaded cells. Decide every editable cell.

Undecided stripes are not clear cells. Mark every editable cell to complete the puzzle. A hint reveals an answer; it is not a deduction tutor. Full controls and persistence details are in the guide.
