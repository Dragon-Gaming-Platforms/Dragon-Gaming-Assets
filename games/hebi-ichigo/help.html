Rules and controls

In white cells, place snakes of exactly five orthogonally connected cells numbered 1 → 2 → 3 → 4 → 5 (head to tail). Different snakes must not share an edge; diagonal contact is allowed. Looking forward from a head in the direction 2 → 1, no other snake may be visible before the next black cell or the board edge.

A black cell's arrow and number describe the first snake number seen along that ray, stopping at another black cell or the edge. A 0 clue means no snake is seen. The number of snakes is not specified; some white cells must stay empty. Explicit Empty is different from unknown. Every white cell must be settled as a number or Empty to finish.

Tap a white cell to select it, then use Empty / 1–5 buttons or number keys. 0 or Space marks Empty; Delete/Backspace restores unknown. Arrows navigate; H gives a deduction and Z undoes. Undo does not refund hints. Select one of three tiers. For a reproducible board, use index.html#t=sho&s=10 (tiers: sho, chuu, gao). Unfinished cells and costs can resume from the menu.

Interface and saves

This guide is not a full translation. Menus, diagnostics and hint prose remain partly Simplified Chinese. Browser storage is optional and game-scoped; progress cannot persist if storage is blocked or full. Fullscreen is optional and may be unavailable in an iframe.
