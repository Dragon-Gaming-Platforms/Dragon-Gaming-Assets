Rules and controls

Sort the stack from smallest at the top to largest at the bottom. Choose a depth k and flip the top k pancakes as one block, reversing their order. In burnt-side puzzles the flip also reverses the facing of every included pancake, and all burnt sides must finish facing down. Tap a pancake’s lower edge to choose and flip that prefix. With the board focused, Up/Down select a depth and Enter/Space flips; number keys 1–9 flip directly. U: undo; H: hint; R: restart. The par is an exact shortest-path distance, not an estimate. A hint reveals an optimal next flip; hinted runs remain marked as assisted and do not earn a new clean completion. Restarting does not remove that assisted status. Completed records and preferences are saved; unfinished stacks restart on reload. The menu lists campaign tiers and random challenges.

Interface and saves

The original interface is Simplified Chinese. This guide is not a full translation. Saves are local to this browser when storage is available. Each game has its own namespaced keys; storage failures should not prevent play. Fullscreen may be unavailable in an iframe and is not required.
