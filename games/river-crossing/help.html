Rules and controls

Move every role and the boat to the right bank. The English panel on the game identifies the numbered tokens, boat capacity and this puzzle’s safety rules.

Drag a token onto the boat to load it. Drag it back to its current bank to unload it. Tap the boat, drag it fully to the opposite bank, or press Space/Enter to cross. Only completed crossings count as moves; loading is free. Invalid or unsafe crossings are rejected.

In ferry mode the boatkeeper must be aboard and counts toward capacity. Crossing alone is allowed. The boatkeeper supervises his current bank, so conflicts there are ignored. In free mode any nonempty legal group may row, but neither bank is supervised. Forbidden pairs must never remain together on an unsupervised bank. For majority puzzles, cannibals may not outnumber missionaries on an unsupervised bank containing missionaries. H / 提示 gives an optimal next cargo from your current position; U / 撤销 undoes; R / 重来 restarts. Completed records are saved, not unfinished crossings.

Interface and saves

The original interface is Simplified Chinese; this guide is not a full translation. Campaign / 战役 selects regular puzzles; 每日 selects a date-based puzzle; 随机 selects a seeded challenge. Browser storage is game-scoped and optional. Storage denial or quota failures must not prevent play; progress cannot persist when storage is unavailable. Fullscreen may be unavailable in an iframe and is not required.
